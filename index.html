About

Tian is a Miami-based artist and musician working across sound and sight.

Known for his work in deep creative circles within art, music & design, Tian has built a reputation on composition, texture, and emotional precision, qualities he expresses in his visual practice.

Working primarily with oils, occasionally delving into mixed media territory, with sealed rose petals, gold leaf, and layered surfaces, Tian creates one-of-one originals that sit between structure and bloom: geometric, deliberate, and deeply sensual at once. Works like “el león de oro,” “and his heart hardened,” and “between structure & bloom” explore power, tenderness, faith, and identity through saturated color and meticulous design.

Each piece is made as a singular artifact — signed, 1 of 1, unframed — intended to live with the collector and grow more complex over time, the way a song does.

Tian lives and works in Miami, Florida.

Practices

  • Music: production / composition / performance
  • Visual Art: oil / acrylic / mixed media
  • Design: technical drafting / drawing

Shipping

Processing

Orders are packed within 3–5 business days. Because each piece is original, availability is limited to one.

Delivery

Works ship insured and tracked. Domestic delivery typically takes 3–7 business days; international 7–21 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are the buyer’s responsibility.

Packaging

Every piece is wrapped in acid-free materials and shipped in a rigid, protective carton or crate.

Framing

All paintings are sold unframed. Framing is optional and available at an additional cost; email me for a quote before you order.

Returns

If a work arrives damaged, contact me within 7 days with photos and I will arrange a repair, replacement, or refund. Because these are original works, other returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery if the piece is unaltered and returned in its original packaging at the buyer’s expense.

Privacy Policy

I collect only the information needed to process and ship your order, such as your name, address, email, and payment details. Payment is arranged directly with you and handled through a secure third-party method; I do not store your full card number.

How information is used

To fulfill orders, respond to messages, and meet legal obligations. I do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time through the contact page.

Terms of Sale

All artwork is the original work of the artist. Purchasing a work transfers ownership of the physical piece only; copyright, including reproduction rights, remains with the artist.

Pricing and payment

Prices are listed in US dollars. Sales are arranged by inquiry: after you reach out, I will confirm availability and send payment instructions. Payment is due in full before the work ships. Colors may vary slightly between screens and the physical work.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the artist’s home jurisdiction.

Commissions

I accept a limited number of one-of-one commissions. Tell me what moves you, and I’ll reply personally to talk through ideas, size, timeline, and pricing. Terms are agreed in writing before any work begins.
